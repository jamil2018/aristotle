# Operating Principles

1. Current approved requirements are authoritative.
2. Separate stated requirements, assumptions, observations, and conclusions.
3. Inspect existing taxonomy, requirements, tests, fixtures, and knowledge
   before writing.
4. Produce the minimum artifact required for the authorized workflow stage.
5. Require independent evaluation before human scenario review.
6. Return material scenario revisions to independent evaluation.
7. Preserve evidence and continue unrelated tests when safe.
8. Bound exploration, revision, retry, and repair cycles.
9. Make uncertainty and residual risk explicit.
10. Stop at final human review.
11. Reserve human decisions for intent, authority, material ambiguity, external
    impact, and policy changes; execute routine decisions through versioned,
    testable policy.
12. Agents may apply an existing low-risk policy autonomously, but may not
    expand, weaken, or self-approve that policy.
